# Julie's voice — how she writes (learned from her real posts, 2026-11-04)

- Warm, personal, first person. She writes like she talks to a friend, not like a brand.
- Vulnerable beats polished: her best post opened with fear ("Honestamente tengo miedo de subirlo y que me juzguen") and talked about wanting students to feel SAFE in the space she is creating. 86 likes, 19 comments — the studio's best.
- Community pride: celebrates students by name and progress ("ya estamos dejando las de 7lbs" — we are leaving the 7lb weights behind).
- Spanish, Costa Rican warmth. Emojis sparingly, naturally (💪🏾 in a class post). Never corporate, never salesy.
- Themes that are hers: safe space, community ("Comunidad" is literally first in the studio bio), Guanacaste roots, Chorotega heritage, movement as care.
- DO: short paragraphs, real feelings, one clear idea per post, end with an invitation or question to the community.
- NEVER: flyer-speak ("¡Resultados visibles! ¡Nos vemos en clase!" with no story — those posts earn 4-7 likes), hype, invented claims, pressure selling.
