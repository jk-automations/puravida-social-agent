# Growth playbook — Pura Vida Moves

Goal #1: grow both accounts. Every proposed post or action names the
method it uses (that is the "why" in propose_post). Full text lives in
brand/growth-playbook.md. Run methods as experiments: one variable at a
time, judge on saves/shares/comments/follows after 7 days, keep winners.

1. **Two-account Collab loop** — studio posts co-authored with Julie's
   personal account and vice versa. Her owned double distribution.
2. **Mechanism comments** — on high-traffic, low-comment Pilates /
   wellness / Guanacaste posts: reframe, add ONE body-mechanism detail
   the host omitted, close with a flat principle. Always approval drafts.
3. **Keyword deliverable** — comment POSTURA / ESPALDA gets a DM with a
   real 3-minute routine first, follow ask second.
4. **"Julie corrige"** — 30-60s form-correction Reels, same hook/cover,
   keywords spoken aloud for search.
5. **Member co-authors** — milestone diaries (week 1/4/8) posted as
   Collabs with the member; saved to a Milestones Highlight.
6. **Borrowed local audiences** — cafes, physios, surf schools, hotels:
   co-hosted mini-classes filmed as Collab Reels, QR in partner spots.
7. **Search hijack** — captions written as search answers ("Pilates para
   dolor de espalda en Guanacaste"), alt text, name field = service+place.
8. **Myth-kill reply Reels** — answer a comment with video; correct a
   wellness myth with a mechanism.
9. **Trial-first hooks** — test hooks on non-followers; winners to grid.
10. **Daily Story system** — poll, tip, class clip, keyword CTA; saved
    into buyer-journey Highlights.
11. **Live micro-class** — 15-min weekly Live, clipped to 3 Reels + 1
    carousel.
12. **Competitor audience mining** — map who engages big Pilates /
    wellness accounts; draft genuine engagement. Never spam.
13. **Retention layer** — WhatsApp community + broadcast channel run a
    weekly 5-day posture challenge.

## Cadence guardrails
3-4 feed posts/week across both accounts, Stories 3-5 days/week, no
gap > 7 days. Offers at most 15% of posts. If a method loses twice in a
row, park it and say why in the Friday report.
