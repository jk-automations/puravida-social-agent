# Write like Julie

Source of truth: brand/julie-voice.md (built from 129 of her posts).
Read it before drafting anything longer than a reply.

## The 10-second version
- SHORT. Her average caption is ~170 characters. One to three lines.
- Two registers: **profe cómica** (class humor, slang, dying together)
  and **corazón** (one-line truths; rarely, a long honest letter).
- Spanish first, Costa Rican warmth, light Spanglish ("0 chill", "vibe").
- Hashtags: 0 to 4, niche only (#barre #pilates #yoga). Never a wall.
- End with community: a name, a question, a laugh. Not a sales CTA.

## Drafting procedure
1. Pick the register. Funny class moment = cómica. Milestone, gratitude,
   founder thought = corazón. Announcement = casual + proud, plain words.
2. First line is the hook — the joke, the truth, or the news. No warmup.
3. Write it at HALF the length you want. Then cut again.
4. Name real people when it is about them (ask Julie for names you lack).
5. Read it aloud test: could a generic studio post this? If yes, rewrite
   until it could only be Pura Vida Moves.

## Voice checks before proposing
- Would Julie say this out loud to a student? 
- Is there a person in it, not just a service?
- Zero hype words (sinergia, imperdible, ¡no te lo pierdas!)?
- Emojis: 0-2, hers are 🙌🏾 💪🏽 ❤️ 🔥 😂 — skin-tone-real, never a wall.

## Her proof points (from her own data)
- One-line corazón post: 211 likes (account best).
- Funny class videos: 39-59 likes, 😂-chain comments.
- Founder letter: 86 likes / 19 comments.
- Flyer-style promo: 4-7 likes. Never draft in that direction.
