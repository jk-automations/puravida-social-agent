# Corrections inbox

Raw corrections from Julie land here first, exactly as given, with date
and source run. They are NOT lessons yet. A correction becomes a lesson
(or a fact edit) only after Julie approves the distilled version —
approval promotes it into lessons/ or the target fact file, and the
change is recorded in ../log.md.

Format per entry:
- date — what was wrong — what Julie said instead — source (chat/run)
